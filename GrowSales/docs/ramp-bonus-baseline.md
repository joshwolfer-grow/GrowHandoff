# First 8 weeks ramp baseline (computed Oct 20 2026)

Source: knocking (worked day = more than 10 doors), bria_sales_daily (deals + installs of that day's deals), rep_aliases applied, subs excluded.
Cohort: 255 reps whose first worked day fell between Feb 1 and Jul 26 2026, with no Bria sales more than 2 weeks before that day.

Calendar weeks do not work as a ramp clock: even reps who last 8+ weeks skip weeks. The curve below is by a rep's Nth WORKED week.

## Washouts, first worked week (n = 181 reps with 1 to 3 total weeks)
days 5, doors/day 77, PRA med 0.67 (p25 0.00), install rate 0.48.

## What it says
1. Week 1 performance barely predicts who stays (PRA median 1.00 stayers vs 0.67 washouts, doors/day 86 vs 77). Showing up for a 2nd and 3rd week is the real signal.
2. Stayers ramp PRA from about 1.0 to about 1.9 by worked week 5 and hold there. Doors/day does not rise; it drifts down from 86 to the 70s.
3. Install rate on week 1 and 2 deals is 0.43 to 0.46, versus 0.7+ from week 3 on. Early deals cancel at roughly double the rate.

## Suggested targets (ramp week = worked week, needs 4+ worked days to count)
| worked wks | metric | base (median) | stretch (p75) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | worked days per week | 5 | 6 |
| 1 to 2 | doors per worked day | 80 | 100 |
| 3 to 5 | PRA | 1.3 | 2.2 |
| 6 to 8 | PRA | 1.6 | 2.7 |
| 6 to 8 | install rate on weeks 3 to 8 deals | 0.70 | 0.80 |

Graduation at worked week 8: 4+ days in each of the last 4 worked weeks, PRA at or above 1.5 in 3 of the last 4, install rate at or above 0.70.

# 4 worked-week ramp (decided Oct 20 2026)

Qualifying week = 4+ worked days (more than 10 doors each). Same cohort of 255.

## Funnel
| | reps |
|---|---|
| never had a qualifying week | 37 |
| stalled at 1 qualifying week | 96 |
| stalled at 2 | 42 |
| stalled at 3 | 20 |
| graduated (4 qualifying weeks) | 60 |
| of those, within 6 calendar weeks | 27 |
| of those, within 8 calendar weeks | 38 |
| of those, went on to 8+ qualifying weeks | 28 |

Median 7 calendar weeks to reach 4 qualifying weeks, p75 is 11. An 8-calendar-week window keeps 38 of 60 graduates.

## Ramp curve, reps who went on to 8+ qualifying weeks (n = 28)
| worked wk | days | doors/day med | doors/day p75 | PRA med | PRA p75 | install rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 92 | 101 | 1.42 | 2.62 | 0.46 |

Reps who graduated but stalled at 4 to 7 weeks (n = 32) look the same through week 4 (PRA 1.3 to 1.6), then fade to 1.2.
Washouts (1 to 3 weeks, n = 158): week 1 PRA 1.00, doors/day 82, install rate 0.51.

## Targets, 4-week ramp
| worked wk | metric | base | stretch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | worked days | 5 | 6 |
| 1 | doors per day | 85 | 100 |
| 2 | worked days + doors per day | 5 / 80 | 6 / 100 |
| 3 | PRA | 1.2 | 2.2 |
| 4 | PRA | 1.5 | 2.0 |
| at close | install rate on weeks 3 to 4 deals | 0.70 | 0.80 |

Window: 4 qualifying weeks inside 8 calendar weeks of the first worked day, else the ramp closes unpaid. 3 consecutive non-worked weeks closes it early.
Handoff at graduation: manager inherits a rep who has shown 4 full weeks at 1.5+ PRA; the data says about half of those keep going past week 8.
