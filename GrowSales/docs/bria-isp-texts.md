# Bria ISP texts and install reminders

Oct 21 2026. For Quaid: per ISP language for the Bria customer text and the install
reminders on the last page of Add Sale (both set under ISP team settings).
Sources: the Canva ISP guides (v2 where one exists) plus live install window data.
Placeholders: {first} {rep} {date} {window}. No dashes anywhere, Bria copies them as typed.

Arrival style by ISP (read the right one off the booking):
- Exact 2 hour window: TDS (8a-10a, 10a-11:30a, 1p-3p, 3p-5p), Ezee (8-10, 10-12, 12-2, 1-3, 3-5), T-Fiber (8-10, 10-12, 1-3, 3-5)
- 4 hour window: i3 (8-12, 10-2, 12-4), Surf (morning 8-12, afternoon 12-4)
- All day: SLIC (8 to 5)
- Tech calls ahead: Lyte 30 min, i3 about 15 min
- ISP schedules later, no date at sale: Ezee presale, Northern Lights, Cityside (Bailey books it at the door by phone)
- Unknown from the guides: Omni, Bluepeak, Glo, GBN (no GBN guide exists)

## Install reminders (rep reads to the customer, last page of Add Sale)

All ISPs
- Someone 18+ must be home the whole window. A missed install goes to the back of the line.
- Keep the rep's number. Texting the rep beats calling the ISP line.
- Never promise mesh or extenders. The tech decides at install.

TDS
- Two hour window, read the exact one off the booking.
- Have them tap Confirm Appointment in the TDS email before you leave.
- Porting a phone number pushes install 10 days out.

Ezee scheduled
- Two hour window. A 4 hour window on the summary means the order failed, redo it.
- Fiber laid across the lawn 2 to 3 days before, buried on install day.
- Screenshot the order summary and text it with your name. Have them scan the scheduling QR.

Ezee presale
- No date. Ezee calls and texts when the area is live, 2 weeks to a month or more.
- They must answer that call. Save the scheduling number via the QR code.

T-Fiber
- Two hour window from the order. Customer must confirm the order within 24 hours or it cancels, do not leave until they do.

i3
- 4 hour window, tech calls about 15 min out, 1 to 2 hour install.
- First month charges 2 days before install. No payment, no install.
- Temp line on the grass 3 to 4 weeks. Phone ports: 10 days out, not Saturday.

Surf
- Morning 8 to 12 or afternoon 12 to 4, never a clock time.
- They must reply Yes to the confirmation text and put a card on file before you add the deal to Bria.
- Line above ground 2 to 3 weeks, slack left for mowing.

SLIC
- All day window, 8 to 5. $20 prepay at signing. $100 no show fee, only mention if asked.
- Care team calls by next business day for the account password.
- Bill on the 1st, due the 20th, autopay is NOT automatic. Cancel Spectrum the day after install, not before.
- Sold after noon means no next day install.

Lyte
- 48 hour confirm call or text, tech calls 30 min before. No answer at the door = no show after 15 min.
- Adult home up to 2 hours. Line on grass 5 to 7 business days.
- Text them the Lyte handout from Enzy.

GFiber
- Outside crew first, then inside install about 90 min on the chosen date.
- Have them install and sign in to the GFiber app before you leave.
- Prior GFiber homes get a self install kit in 2 to 3 business days instead.

Glo
- Pre drop text 2 to 7 days before, nobody home needed. What to expect email the day before.
- Phone port needs install 10+ days out. Missed install: Glo tries 5 times, then it comes back to you.

Omni
- Drop, inside run and router all one visit. Buried lines come back within 10 days, keep the mower off.
- Nothing over 3 days out without flagging your lead.

Bluepeak
- Customer MUST text back to confirm the order the same night or it cancels. Stay until they do.
- Set up autopay with them on the spot. Supervisor survey within 2 days, pre drop a few days before, burial up to 10 days after.

Northern Lights
- NLF schedules the install, about 3 weeks: 14 days permitting, flags, drop, then the booking call.
- Billing starts on install day.

Cityside
- Call Bailey to book before leaving the door. Aerial = 2 visits, underground = 3 (consultation first).
- About 4 hours. Have them save 949-779-2489. Three missed contacts and Cityside cancels.

Ting
- Trench crew 4 to 10 days before with no notice. Email plus 2 texts before install.
- Use promo Welcome (own router) or Home12 (rented).
