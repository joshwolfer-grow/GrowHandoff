## 4. Numbers that disagree between screens

Ranked by how often someone will see two different values. Line numbers are in `index.html` unless stated.

1. **PRA denominator.** The ten-door rule (sale-days do not count as worked) holds on KPIs (`_kwBase` 19747) and the daily manager card (`_mgrWeekSales` 12140), but NOT on the Weeks page and drills (`growWorkedReps` 5144 adds sellers), the weekly summary text/image (`_praDayCount` 2851), the rep email (`buildPraGoals` api/weekly-rep-email.js:930) or the meeting deck script (`meeting-deck-stats.py:86`). A rep who sold with no knock row raises PRA's denominator on some screens and not others.
2. **Sold source.** Weekly summary (`computeWeek` 12637, `buildWeeklySummaryReport` ~12475) and api/weekly-summary.js count Sequifi deals; everything else counts Bria. Coach overview (`_repDaySold` 3878) picks whichever pipe is richest per week.
3. **BRIA_EXCLUDE** (cross-rep duplicate stopgap) is applied only in the browser (3091). The rep email and deck script read Bria raw, so Will Gabor's Oct 3 week is 25 in the app and 30 in the email. Belongs in bria-sync, not the client.
4. **Knocking dedupe in the email.** `mergeKnockingSources` in weekly-rep-email.js:170 SUMS overlapping sr_activity + timetrack rows (and never selects `method`); the app takes MAX. Doors and sold are inflated in the email for any rep with both sources. Same in meeting-deck-stats.py:47.
5. **Sub exclusion.** Email reads `rep_flags.is_sub` only; the app's `growIsSub` (2321) also honours `roster_status` and the Bria-team rule. `repIsSub` (old, is_sub only) is still called at 11190 and 19131. `computeWeek` and `growWeekFunnel` apply no sub filter at all.
6. **Intake accounts** (Daxton Day, Nick Boyd) are filtered from `knocking_daily` only. Raw knocking feeds the Knock board ISP view (8918) and Custom Report (23150) unfiltered; their deals are excluded on KPI pages only.
7. **Close rate** is computed four ways: `_crCell` (guarded, day-paired), knock board card 9672 (can exceed 100%), drill 14276 (0 instead of blank), email :501.
8. **Week boundary in the email** (weekly-rep-email.js:279) mixes a local Sunday with `toISOString()`, so after about 5 pm Mountain it lands on the wrong week.
9. meeting-deck-stats.py hardcodes teams instead of reading `__bria_teams__`.

Consistent everywhere checked: Completed IR = inst/(inst+canc); Install Rate = inst/sold; report-lag THRU clip; teams from `__bria_teams__` in app, meeting board and email; all in-app week helpers are Sunday-anchored; in-app knocking dedupe handles the method overlap.
